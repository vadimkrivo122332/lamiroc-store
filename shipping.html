Delivery & Shipping Policy

1. Manufacturing & Processing Time
Because Lamiroc OÜ manufactures technical parkas and outerwear under order through our partner facilities, fulfillment timelines depend on batch production schedules and chosen material specifications. Standard production runs typically require preparation and tailoring before dispatch.

2. Shipping Destinations & Methods
We coordinate secure shipping for individual retail orders and corporate team batches across Estonia and the broader European region. Couriers and tracking details are provided upon dispatch from our production logistics hub.

3. Shipping Costs & Delivery Estimates
Shipping rates are calculated based on destination, package weight, and order volume during the quoting or checkout process. Estimated transit times vary by region (typically 3–7 business days within the EU once production is finalized).

4. Contact & Logistics Support
For questions regarding your active shipment or specific delivery timelines, please contact our support team at Support@lamiroc.online or call +372 5885 5076.
