Privacy Policy

1. Data Collection
Lamiroc OÜ collects personal data (name, phone, email, delivery address) voluntarily provided during order checkout, quote requests, or fabric swatch requests.

2. Purpose of Processing
Data is used exclusively to fulfill your orders, manage communication, issue invoices, and handle logistics.

3. Data Protection (GDPR)
We comply with strict EU privacy standards. Your data is never shared with third parties, except courier delivery services and Estonian tax authorities as required by law.
