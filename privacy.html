Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Lamiroc OÜ complies with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) regarding the collection, use, and protection of your personal data.

1. Data Controller & Contact Information

The data controller responsible for your personal data is Lamiroc OÜ (Reg. code: 17380509, address: Telliskivi tn 60a/5, Tallinn, 10412, Estonia).
If you have any questions, requests, or concerns regarding your data protection, you can directly contact our data protection responsible representative at: Support@lamiroc.online.

2. Data Collection & Purpose of Processing

We collect personal data (such as full name, email address, phone number, shipping address, and payment details) voluntarily provided by you during order checkout, quote inquiries, or fabric swatch requests.
This data is processed strictly to fulfill your orders, manage customer support communications, issue invoices, handle logistics, and comply with legal accounting obligations.

3. Data Retention Period

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, including legal, accounting, or reporting requirements:
- Transactional & Financial Records: Invoices and order data are kept for 7 years in accordance with Estonian accounting and tax legislation.
- Customer Support & Inquiries: Communication logs and general inquiries are retained for up to 2 years unless a longer contractual relationship requires otherwise.
- Consent-based marketing data: Retained until you withdraw your consent.

4. Your Rights Under GDPR ("Right to be Forgotten")

Under the GDPR, you hold robust rights regarding your personal data. You have the right to:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request the deletion of your personal data when it is no longer necessary for the purposes collected (subject to statutory tax retention limits).
  • Right to Restrict or Object: Object to or limit the processing of your data under specific conditions.
  • Right to Data Portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.

To exercise any of these rights, please send an email to Support@lamiroc.online.

5. Data Sharing & Third Parties

Your data is kept confidential and is never sold or shared with commercial third parties. We share data only with strictly necessary partners required to operate our business, such as authorized courier services (for shipping orders) and Estonian tax/legal authorities as required by law.
