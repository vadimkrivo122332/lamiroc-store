Returns & Refunds Policy

Clear arrangements for changing your mind, cancelling custom orders, reporting a fault, and receiving a refund. Mandatory statutory consumer rights under EU law are not reduced by this policy.

1. Standard Distance Purchases & 14-Day Right of Withdrawal

For standard non-customized stock items purchased by EU consumers, you have the statutory right to cancel and return your order without giving any reason within 14 days after receiving the goods. To exercise this right, notify us clearly by email at Support@lamiroc.online or by post to Lamiroc OÜ, Telliskivi tn 60a/5, Tallinn, 10412, Estonia.

2. Custom-Made & Personalized Garments (Cancellation & Refund Rules)

Under EU consumer protection regulations, items manufactured according to consumer specifications or clearly personalized (such as garments featuring custom corporate logo embroidery, individual text branding, or tailor-made dimensions unique to the client) are exempt from the standard 14-day change-of-mind return right once production has started.

Transparent Pre-Production Cancellation Mechanism:
- Before Production Start: If you cancel your custom order before design layout approval and before raw material cutting/tailoring has begun, you will receive a 100% full refund of all funds paid, issued within 14 days.
- After Production Start: If you cancel after design approval and after manufacturing/tailoring has commenced, we reserve the right to deduct reasonable, verifiable expenses already incurred for specialized materials and labor directly allocated to your custom order before issuing a partial refund of the remaining balance.

3. Condition of Goods & Return Shipping Costs

For eligible standard returns, items must be unworn, unwashed, and complete with original tags. You may inspect and try on garments as you would in a physical shop. The buyer pays the direct cost of return shipping for change-of-mind returns. No general restocking fees apply.

4. Refund Processing Timeline

Approved refunds are processed without undue delay and strictly within 14 days from the day we are informed of your decision to cancel (for pre-production cancellations) or from the day we receive the returned goods / proof of return (for physical returns). Refunds are returned via the original payment method (secure payment link/card/invoice payment) with no additional fees.

5. Faulty or Defective Goods (2-Year EU Legal Guarantee)

Regardless of customization, all items are covered by the mandatory minimum two-year EU legal guarantee of conformity. If a garment arrives with manufacturing defects, incorrect specifications, or flaws, contact us immediately at Support@lamiroc.online. Valid claims entitle you to a free repair, replacement, or appropriate refund, and Lamiroc OÜ covers all reasonable return shipping costs for defective items.

6. Business-to-Business (B2B) Purchases

Corporate and business buyers purchasing with a VAT number do not possess statutory consumer withdrawal rights. B2B order cancellations and adjustments are handled strictly in accordance with the written commercial agreement or quotation terms.
